[Start]
Narrator: The tavern door opens. -> TavernChoice

[TavernChoice]
1. Approach the stranger. -> StrangerPath
2. Speak with the merchant. -> MerchantPath

[StrangerPath]
Stranger: The mountain keeps its secrets. -> Rejoin

[MerchantPath]
Merchant: Supplies are expensive today. -> Rejoin

[Rejoin]
Narrator: Both paths return you to the center of the room. -> LoopChoice

[LoopChoice]
1. Ask someone else. -> TavernChoice
2. Leave the tavern. -> Exit

[Exit]
Narrator: The night air is cold. -> End
